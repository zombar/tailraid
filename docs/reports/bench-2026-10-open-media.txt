Reading of the numbers
- A 4 MiB chunk fetched alone (the seeks: k shards in parallel, decode, hash, cache
  write) costs 0.15-0.35 s on this tailnet. The head of a file costs 0.7 s on both
  gateways: chunk 0 is a sequential read, so File.ReadAt started four prefetches (16 MiB)
  before fetching chunk 0, and the client's chunk shared the holders' slots and the links
  with them. Warm reads cost the round trip only (2 ms local, 30 ms across the tailnet).
- /api/status walks every chunk (44k) on the request: 0.26 s on the Mac, 0.44 s on
  minixps, every 5 s per open tab.
- A cold listing of /music on minixps took 5.5 s: ls resolved each folder's cover by
  reading the tags of its audio children in line (jockey's cover cache was warm).

What PR A1 (open-fast) changes, each pinned by an invariant
- I-203 readahead: starts after the client's read is served, reaches 8 MiB (two chunks
  at 4 MiB) at background priority, and a seek cancels the unjoined prefetches.
- I-204 cache: the chunk is served from memory as soon as it is verified; the 4 MiB
  disk write runs behind the Put.
- I-205 status: a refresher builds the report; a request serves the latest build;
  walk_ms is in the report.
- I-165 covers: a listing reads no file; the folder's thumbnail request resolves the
  cover once per folder version.
